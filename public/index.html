Questions

Good to know

Who can subscribe?

Licensed REALTORS® who are members of Greater Vancouver REALTORS®. You confirm your membership when you set up your profile.

When is the report ready?

Early each month, shortly after the board releases the previous month's statistics. You get an email the moment it is live.

Does my link change every month?

No. Your link stays the same and always shows the newest report, so you can keep it in your email signature or bio.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from your account. Your report stays live until the end of the period you paid for.
